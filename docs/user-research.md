# UnseenLab — User Research

**Honesty rule: only confirmed information is recorded as confirmed. Templates below are placeholders for real sessions — nothing here is fabricated, and no test session has been claimed.**

## Initial design participant (confirmed)

- Approximately 17 years old.
- High-school senior.
- Has disclosed ADHD (self-disclosed; UnseenLab never uses diagnosis as a design preset).
- Aspiring mountain biker; interested in engineering.
- Reports learning differently from how material is normally presented in class.
- Reports difficulty understanding Modern Physics in AP Physics 2.
- Said demonstrations and laboratory examples did not provide enough interactivity to build a full understanding.
- Strongly values **interactivity and animation**.

## Confirmed preferences (from the initial design interview)

1. Interactivity in labs is a confirmed preference.
2. Animation is a confirmed preference.
3. Need for broader, universal accessibility of representations is a confirmed direction (participant's own experience, generalized carefully — never claimed for all learners with ADHD).

## How this shaped the product (design revision log)

| Change | Rationale | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Animation is the visual center of the lab | Confirmed preference for animation | Initial design participant |
| Every representation (animation/graph/equation/causal/plain language) is a learner choice | Confirmed preference for interactivity; no single fixed interface | Initial design participant |
| Prediction-before-run workflow | Interactivity requirement: learner manipulates meaningfully before seeing results | Initial design participant + rubric |
| Adaptation offers instead of automatic changes | Learner control is the core design principle | Initial design participant + accessibility review |
| No diagnosis-based presets | Never assume preferences from identity | Product principle (all learners) |
