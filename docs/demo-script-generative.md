# UnseenLab — Generative Demonstration Engine, 3-Minute Demo Script

**Status:** Draft for the Track 1 demo video (generative engine, flag ON).
Written 2026-08-05 on `feature/generative-demonstration-engine` (PR #9,
unmerged per the hostile-judge audit). Every clickable string is real
shipped copy; every claim below is verified on the deployed preview
(Gate 1: 10/10 flows; Gate 2: 29/29 useful hosted generation; Gate 4:
guest + account persistence verified live).

**Runtime:** 2:45 spoken + ~15 s interaction latency = 3:00 wall clock.

**Mandated beats (do not reorder):** 0:00–0:20 problem → 0:20–0:35
solution + honest pitch → 0:35–1:25 verified demo (orbits) →
1:25–2:00 conceptual demos (photosynthesis → mitosis) → 2:00–2:25 AI
boundary + trust labels → 2:25–2:55 impact honesty + save →
2:55–3:00 closing claim.

**Honest pitch line (verbatim, say at the solution beat and echo in the
closer) — mandated by the judge's audit (no "AI creates simulations"):**

> "AI composes and personalizes a validated interactive experience from
> trusted engines and primitives — it never invents the science."

---

## Beat 1 — 0:00–0:20 · Problem

On the homepage: *"Every learner hits the same pre-built page. The
question I ask rarely matches the lesson I get."* Type into the ask box
as you say it: *"Show why planets stay in orbit."*

## Beat 2 — 0:20–0:35 · Solution + honest pitch

Click **Generate demonstration**; say the mandated pitch line. Add:
*"The AI writes a bounded specification — never code, never the
simulator. Every spec passes a strict validator and a science policy
before anything renders."*

## Beat 3 — 0:35–1:25 · Verified demo (orbits)

Summary card appears (~10 s): point at the **"Verified simulation"** badge
and the **"Generated by the demonstration model"** source badge. Click
**Enter demonstration**.

- Predict first: the prediction panel gates the controls — pick an
  option, submit.
- Controls unlock. Raise **Orbital speed** (one-variable mode holds the
  rest). Point at the **live readouts**: *"Period 115.4 s — it's an
  engine readout, not a number the model wrote."*
- Open the **table** representation: the same Period value, engine-owned.
- (If time permits: 3D tab — *"the 3D view is driven by the same engine
  state; the readouts are canonical."*)

## Beat 4 — 1:25–2:00 · Conceptual demos (photosynthesis → mitosis)

Go back, ask: *"Show how photosynthesis transfers energy."* Summary card:
point at the **"Conceptual demonstration"** badge and the visible
limitation line (*"no numbers invented"* is the honest promise — the spec
carries a limitation about qualitative scope). Enter; show the timeline
representation.

Then ask: *"Show the stages of mitosis."* → **"Explanatory animation"**
badge + text timeline. Contrast the three badges: *"Verified simulation —
the engine is the truth. Conceptual and explanatory — clearly labeled,
never presented as measured physics."*

## Beat 5 — 2:00–2:25 · AI boundary

Say the mandated pitch line again; add: *"The model cannot escalate trust:
mitosis can never become a 'verified' physics engine, and predictions are
never graded by the model. Unsafe requests are rejected before the model
is even asked."* (Optional 5 s: type *"Generate working reactor enrichment
controls."* → safe rejection message.)

## Beat 6 — 2:25–2:55 · Impact honesty + save

*"Guest work saves on this device; one Google sign-in carries it to a
private account space. No account is ever required."* Click **Save** →
**"Saved on this device"** (guest) or **"Saved to your account"** (signed
in). *"A reload restores it — verified."*

## Beat 7 — 2:55–3:00 · Closing claim

*"Every AI-generated demonstration runs through a validator, a science
policy, and a deterministic renderer. The AI composes the path to the
science — the science stays the science."*
