## How to fill a row (after a real session only)

1. **Observation** — what the participant did or said (verbatim where possible), from the facilitator notes and the exported research JSON.
2. **Evidence** — pointer to the export: session id, event types, timestamps.
3. **Product change** — the smallest honest change built in response.
4. **Reason** — why this observation led to this change.
5. **Before** — what the product did before the change.
6. **After** — what the product does after the change.
7. **Verification** — how the change was checked (follow-up session, replay of evidence, reviewer).
